OVERVIEW: Take a Screenshot of the Target

USAGE: taptaptap screenshot <dest-path> [--udid <udid>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <dest-path>             The destination file path to write to, or - (dash) to
                          write to stdout.

OPTIONS:
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
