OVERVIEW: Describes Accessibility Information at a point on the screen

USAGE: taptaptap ui describe-point <x> <y> [--udid <udid>] [--nested]

ARGUMENTS:
  <x>                     The X coordinate.
  <y>                     The Y coordinate.

OPTIONS:
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --nested                Will report data in the newer nested format, rather
                          than the flat one.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
