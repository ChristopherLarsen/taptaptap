OVERVIEW: Perform a low-level point-to-point drag using explicit touch move
events.

USAGE: taptaptap ui drag <x-start> <y-start> <x-end> <y-end> [--duration <duration>] [--steps <steps>] [--pre-delay <pre-delay>] [--post-delay <post-delay>] [--udid <udid>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <x-start>               The X coordinate of the starting point.
  <y-start>               The Y coordinate of the starting point.
  <x-end>                 The X coordinate of the ending point.
  <y-end>                 The Y coordinate of the ending point.

OPTIONS:
  --duration <duration>   Duration of the drag movement in seconds. (default:
                          0.6)
  --steps <steps>         Number of touch move events to emit during the drag.
                          (default: 60)
  --pre-delay <pre-delay> Delay before starting the drag in seconds.
  --post-delay <post-delay>
                          Delay after completing the drag in seconds.
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
