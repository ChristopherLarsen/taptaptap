OVERVIEW: Multi Tap On the Screen (default: double tap)

USAGE: taptaptap ui multi-tap <x> <y> [--count <count>] [--duration <duration>] [--pause <pause>] [--udid <udid>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <x>                     The X coordinate.
  <y>                     The Y coordinate.

OPTIONS:
  --count <count>         Number of taps (default: 2). (default: 2)
  --duration <duration>   Press duration per tap, in seconds.
  --pause <pause>         Pause between taps in seconds (default: 0.1).
                          (default: 0.1)
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
