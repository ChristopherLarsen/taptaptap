OVERVIEW: Perform a pinch gesture

USAGE: taptaptap ui pinch <x> <y> <scale> [--duration <duration>] [--radius <radius>] [--udid <udid>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <x>                     X coordinate of pinch center.
  <y>                     Y coordinate of pinch center.
  <scale>                 Scale factor (>1.0 = zoom in, <1.0 = zoom out).

OPTIONS:
  --duration <duration>   Duration in seconds. (default: 0.5)
  --radius <radius>       Initial finger distance from center in pixels.
                          (default: 100.0)
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
