OVERVIEW: Record the target's screen to a mp4 video file

USAGE: taptaptap video <output-file> [--udid <udid>] [--fps <fps>] [--quality <quality>] [--scale <scale>] [--duration <duration>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <output-file>           mp4 file to output the video to.

OPTIONS:
  --udid <udid>           The UDID of the simulator. Defaults to the sole
                          booted simulator.
  --fps <fps>             Frames per second (1-30, default: 10) (default: 10)
  --quality <quality>     Quality factor (1-100) controlling bitrate (default:
                          80) (default: 80)
  --scale <scale>         Scale factor (0.1-1.0, default: 1.0) (default: 1.0)
  --duration <duration>   Stop and save automatically after this many seconds
                          (greater than 0, up to 3600); no signal needed. Not
                          an idb option.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
